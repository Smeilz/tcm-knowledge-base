# Cough — Treatment Scheme

**Category:** Respiratory
**Source:** Lednev Electroacupuncture Atlas

## Point Prescription by Body Region

### Chest
- P2 Yun-Men — subclavicular fossa, 6 cun lateral to midline
- E18 Ru-Gen — 5th intercostal space, below nipple
- VC17 Tan-Zhong — midline, level with 4th intercostal space

### Back
- V13 Fei-Shu — Back-Shu of Lungs, 1.5 cun lateral to T3
- VG14 Da-Zhui — below C7 spinous process

### Upper Limb
- P5 Chi-Ze — cubital crease, radial side of biceps tendon
- P7 Lie-Que — 1.5 cun above wrist crease, radial side

### Lower Limb
- E36 Zu-San-Li — 3 cun below knee, 1 finger-breadth lateral to tibia
