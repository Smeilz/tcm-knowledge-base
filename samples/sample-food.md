# Rice (粳米) — Jing Mi

## TCM Properties
- **Temperature:** Neutral
- **Taste:** Sweet
- **Meridians:** Spleen, Stomach

## Actions
- Tonifies Spleen Qi, strengthens digestion
- Harmonizes the Stomach
- Generates body fluids, alleviates thirst

## Dietary Therapy
- **Spleen Qi deficiency:** rice congee with dates and astragalus
- **Stomach Yin deficiency:** thin rice porridge with lily bulb
- **Post-illness recovery:** plain congee to restore digestive function

## Contraindications
- Excess dampness patterns — use in moderation
