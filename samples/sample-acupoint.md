# P10 Yu-Ji (LU 10)

**Meridian:** P — Lungs (Hand Tai-Yin)
**Classification:** Ying-Spring point (Fire element)

## Location
At the midpoint of the 1st metacarpal bone, on the border between palmar and dorsal skin.

## Functions
- Clears Lung heat
- Benefits the throat
- Descends rebellious Qi

## Indications
- Lung heat: cough with yellow sticky sputum, hemoptysis, dyspnea
- Throat heat: sore throat, dry throat with thirst, loss of voice
- Middle Jiao disorders: vomiting, epigastric pain

## Needling
Perpendicular insertion 0.5–0.8 cun

- [[bronchitis]] — respiratory category
