# Bai Zhi (白芷) — Dahurian Angelica Root

## Actions & Indications
- Disperses wind-cold, opens nasal passages — headache, nasal congestion, toothache
- Reduces swelling, expels pus — early-stage sores and abscesses
- Dries dampness, stops vaginal discharge — cold-damp leukorrhea

## Dosage
3–10 g in decoction

## Contraindications
Use with caution in Yin-deficiency heat patterns
